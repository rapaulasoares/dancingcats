# Question Matrix

69 questions. Each main interview offers 8 questions and allows 4. Professor Roberto's second interview offers 4 and allows 2.

**No question may assume knowledge the player does not have yet.** Two mechanisms enforce this:

- **Follow-up questions** appear only after the answer that makes them possible (T9 after T4).
- **Knowledge-gated questions** appear only once the player has met the fact they refer to. The four rat questions (C4, C7, A5, S7) appear only after the player has seen rats: the Fish Market and Fishing Community scenes show them on arrival, and RES-06, RES-08, SQ-01 and SQ-03 also count. M6 appears only after the player has read about the algae in the harbor master's log (HAR-08) or the newspaper archive (SQ-09). A hidden question does not count against the player; the interview simply offers fewer choices at that moment.

The **Class** column is hidden from players. Questions are displayed in the order listed, which deliberately mixes strong and weak ones. Short questions are sometimes the strong ones, and reasonable-sounding ones are sometimes weak.

Responses are exact game text. "—" means no evidence is unlocked; the answer is still kept in the Interview Record.

## Investigator Tips

Rules: a tip appears the first time a team chooses each weak class; never more than one tip per interview; at most five per game; never after a strong or useful question.

| Trigger | Tip text |
|---|---|
| Vague | "Broad questions often produce broad answers." |
| Opinion | "Try asking what someone OBSERVED instead of asking them to guess the cause." |
| Leading | "Careful — your question suggested an answer. Could you ask it more neutrally?" |
| Assumption | "That question took something for granted. What do you actually know so far?" |
| Irrelevant | "That answer didn't give you much evidence. Try asking something more specific." |
| Humorous (C2) | "Funny question. Not very useful evidence, though." |
| Humorous (R2, J5, T5) | "That one was for fun. You have a limited number of questions — choose the next one carefully." |
