# Evidence Matrix

64 items. The **Supports / Weakens / Relevance** columns are internal only and are never shown to players.

## Competing hypotheses

| | Hypothesis | Player-facing wording |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Contagious disease | People are passing an illness to one another. |
| **B** | Rats | The increase in rats brought a disease. |
| **C** | Supernatural cause | Something beyond the natural world is affecting Vila do Sol. |
| **D** | Environmental contamination | Something in the environment has entered the food chain. |
| **E** | Natural problem in the bay | A change in temperature, algae, or similar. |

Every wrong hypothesis has clues that make it attractive and separate clues that let the player eliminate it. Section "Elimination map" at the end shows both sides for each hypothesis.

Discovery: **Hotspot** = tapping an object in the scene. **Qn** = asking that question (see the Question Matrix). **Auto** = delivered by the Science Center message.

Evidence text presents information only. No item states a conclusion.

Other clues that lead nowhere: MKT-06 (prices), SQ-04 (football poster), TUC-07 (toy toucan), TUC-01 (company statement), and the answers to irrelevant and humorous questions.

Pacing of elimination: the attractive clues for A, B, C and E are mostly hotspots found early and easily. The eliminating clues are mostly behind good questions or arrive with the lab results. A player who asks weak questions therefore ends with several hypotheses still open, which is the intended consequence.

## Economic dependency (added later)

The factory is neither villain nor innocent: a whole town depends on its jobs, and some of the people who depend on it are also the ones who are sick. Marcos Costa is the character who is both.

| ECO-02 | The Costa family and the factory | Town Square | Marcos Costa | Witness statement | His father, he and his brother work there. "Half this town depends on those jobs. If the factory closes, what happens to us?" | K1 | Medium (context) |
| ECO-03 | Marcos Costa's household health | Town Square | Marcos Costa | Witness statement | His wife's hands went numb in March; his father has the same; he has nothing so far. | K2 | High |
| ECO-04 | Marcos Costa's family meals | Town Square | Marcos Costa | Witness statement | Fish nearly every day, caught off the rocks at weekends. | K3 | High |
| ECO-05 | Protective equipment at the plant | Town Square | Marcos Costa | Witness statement | Gloves, mask, safety glasses and boots, checked every morning. | K5 | Medium |

TUC-08 (chemicals in production) now also says that some catalysts and additives contain heavy metals, and that the company says it follows every rule and equips every employee with protective equipment. That is the clearer link between the company and a heavy metal; it is not proof, because the protective equipment protects workers, not the bay.

Marcos's question K4 ("Do you think Tucano Industrial is responsible?") carries the line "Those fishermen keep blaming the factory. Do they know how many families would lose their jobs if it closed?" It is an opinion answer, so it unlocks no evidence.
